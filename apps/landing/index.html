Deterministic, not a black box

A floor from your costs

Enter your unit cost once. The floor is the highest of your minimum price and the price that still covers cost, marketplace fees and VAT. Strategies stop at the floor; a price that would still land below it is rejected, not corrected — and the check runs again before every single price is sent.

“Why this price” for every price

Every price change and every rejected price is stored with its steps: which inputs were checked, what the strategy wanted, which bound applied; a price left unchanged keeps its reason. Open a price and read why — in English or German.

Three stop switches

  • Your kill switch. Stops every price change for the whole account or one storefront. You press it; only you release it.
  • Automatic halt on suspicious data. When competitor prices on a storefront shift in a way that looks like a data error, prices derived from competitors stop. Fixed and margin-based prices keep working.
  • Distrust of the channel. When the price a marketplace applies differs from the one we sent by exactly the VAT rate — a sign that the price basis is wrong — every price on that storefront stops until a person releases it.

Shadow mode: watch for a week

Connect a marketplace and watch. In shadow mode the system cannot change your prices — physically: every write is recorded as held in the database and never leaves for the marketplace. You see each decision it would have made and why; you switch to live when you are ready, and back with one click.

It knows your stock, too

A repricer you can leave running has to know what you actually have. repracer keeps stock in sync across channels: one pool per product, a buffer per channel, orders reserve and lower what the channels show. We do not own your stock — your system sends it to us through an API or a file. In shadow mode stock writes are held too.
